About ROLCHA

Ray of Light Church Tirana Association is a Christian ministry based in Tirana, Albania, dedicated to evangelism, discipleship, church planting, leadership development and compassionate community outreach.

Pastor Gentjan Dervishaj

Pastor Gentjan Dervishaj serves in Christian ministry in Albania, encouraging local churches and international partnerships to share the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Bless Albania

Bless Albania mobilizes churches and Christian partners for evangelism, discipleship, leadership development and support for vulnerable children and families.

BAM

Bless Albania & Israel Ministries (BAM) encourages prayer, ministry cooperation and international Christian partnerships.
